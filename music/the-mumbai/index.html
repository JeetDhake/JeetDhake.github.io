The Mumbai — Indie by Jeet Dhake

The Mumbai is a indie work by Jeet Dhake, an Indian music producer from Vadodara, Gujarat, India.

What is The Mumbai?

The Mumbai is a song inspired by Bollywood cinema, particularly gritty gangster films. Imagined as the soundtrack to the lives of those characters and their world of power, money, and living life without limits, the track combines low orchestral brass stabs, trumpets, acoustic drums, and sirens to create a dark, raw, cinematic atmosphere. It captures both sides of that lifestyle—the danger and violence on one side, and the extravagant, fearless, almost glamorous way of living on the other.

Indie

Who created The Mumbai?

The Mumbai was created and produced by Jeet Dhake.
