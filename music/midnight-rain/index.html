Midnight Rain — Future Bass by Jeet Dhake

Midnight Rain is a future bass work by Jeet Dhake, an Indian music producer from Vadodara, Gujarat, India.

What is Midnight Rain?

Midnight Rain is a fusion of multiple genres, blending an orchestral intro with a cinematic, trailer-like build-up. It gradually evolves into a powerful future bass drop with an electric guitar lead, filled with intricate arrangements and layered sound design. The track then builds toward a second, even more intense climax, before abruptly falling into silence—leaving the ending feeling almost like the aftermath of something monumental, where the silence itself becomes part of the music.
