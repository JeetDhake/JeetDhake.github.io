Rise From Ashes — Cinematic Music by Jeet Dhake

Rise From Ashes is a cinematic music work by Jeet Dhake, an Indian music producer from Vadodara, Gujarat, India.

What is Rise From Ashes?

Rise from Ashes is a cinematic track that begins with deep, low brass tones, creating a heavy and reflective atmosphere that feels like the quiet aftermath of a monumental journey. Gradually, denser strings and a powerful orchestral theme emerge, building the feeling of returning home after overcoming immense challenges and achieving victory. In the second half, soaring strings and increasingly powerful brass arrangements lift the track into a triumphant crescendo, with each layer growing louder and more intense. Just as the tension reaches its peak, everything suddenly falls into a brief moment of silence—before erupting into a massive drop driven by a heavy, growling electric guitar. The contrast between silence, orchestral grandeur, and raw guitar energy gives Rise from Ashes a sense of resilience, triumph, and a hard-earned return.

Who Created Rise From Ashes?

Rise From Ashes was created and produced by Jeet Dhake.
