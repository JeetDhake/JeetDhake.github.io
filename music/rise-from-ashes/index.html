Rise From Ashes — Cinematic Music by Jeet Dhake

Rise From Ashes is a cinematic music work by Jeet Dhake, an Indian music producer from Vadodara, Gujarat, India.

What is Rise From Ashes?

Rise From Ashes is a cinematic composition representing Jeet Dhake's interest in dramatic, emotional and soundtrack-oriented music.

The work explores the idea of overcoming adversity, transformation and rebuilding after difficult moments. The title represents the concept of turning destruction or hardship into strength and renewal.

Who Created Rise From Ashes?

Rise From Ashes was created and produced by Jeet Dhake.

Genre

Cinematic Music

The track sits within Jeet Dhake's broader interest in cinematic composition, soundtrack music, electronic production and emotional instrumental music.

Why Rise From Ashes Matters

Rise From Ashes is one of Jeet Dhake's highlighted cinematic works. It represents an important creative direction for him beyond traditional electronic music production.

Jeet is interested in creating music that can support a visual story, character, scene or emotional moment.
