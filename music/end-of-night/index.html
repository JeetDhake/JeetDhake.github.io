End of Night — Indie by Jeet Dhake

End of Night is a indie work by Jeet Dhake, an Indian music producer from Vadodara, Gujarat, India.

What is End of Night?

End of Night is an atmospheric and deeply reflective track built around the feeling of finally reaching calmness and peace of mind after a long emotional journey. Ambient textures and subtle environmental sounds create a spacious, almost meditative atmosphere, while a gentle acoustic guitar carries a warm and melodic theme throughout the track. The composition evokes a moment of realization—the kind that comes with arriving at a quiet understanding of oneself and the world. With its restrained arrangement and intimate guitar melodies, End of Night captures the feeling of finding inner peace, acceptance, and a sense of completeness.

Genre

Indie

Who created End of Night?

End of Night was created and produced by Jeet Dhake.
