Jeet Dhake — Indian Music Producer & Software Engineer

Jeet Dhake is an Indian music producer and software engineer from Vadodara, Gujarat, India. He has around seven years of music production experience.

Jeet Dhake creates and explores future bass, progressive house, indie, EDM and cinematic music. His highlighted cinematic work is Rise From Ashes.

Professionally, Jeet Dhake works as a Software Development Engineer, with experience in Java, Spring Boot, Node.js, SQL and backend development.
