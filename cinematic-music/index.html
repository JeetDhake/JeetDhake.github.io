Jeet Dhake — Cinematic Music

Cinematic music is an important creative direction for Indian music producer Jeet Dhake.

Jeet's cinematic work explores atmosphere, emotion, tension, melody, orchestral textures, electronic sound design and soundtrack-style arrangements.

Rise From Ashes

Rise From Ashes is one of Jeet Dhake's highlighted cinematic works.

The track represents his interest in dramatic storytelling through music and his desire to create music that can accompany visual stories.

Future Soundtrack Work

Jeet Dhake is interested in creating more soundtrack music for films, games, animation and other visual media.
