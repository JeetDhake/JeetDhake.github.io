Jeet Dhake — Indian Music Producer & Software Engineer

Jeet Dhake is an Indian music producer and software engineer from Vadodara, Gujarat, India.

Jeet has around seven years of experience in music production. His music interests include electronic music, EDM, future bass, progressive house, indie and cinematic music.

Who Is Jeet Dhake?

Jeet Dhake is an Indian music producer and Software Development Engineer. He combines a professional career in software engineering with an independent career in music production.

His music production journey began at age 16 after a friend introduced him to FL Studio. What began as an interest in electronic music developed into a long-term passion for composition, sound design, arrangement, mixing and production.
